About Topmost Fitness

Your goals. Your training. Your progress.

At Topmost Fitness, we believe fitness is more than just working out — it’s about becoming stronger, moving better, feeling confident, and building a body that supports the life you want to live.

Led by Moses Faith, a Certified Personal Trainer and Strength & Conditioning Coach with 4 years of experience, Topmost Fitness provides personalized training built around individual goals, fitness levels, abilities, and progress.

Whether your goal is weight loss, muscle building, strength development, improved mobility, athletic performance, or general fitness, every training approach is built with purpose.
